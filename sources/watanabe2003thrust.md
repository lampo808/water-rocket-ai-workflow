## What the source supports

From the English abstract only:

- Thrust stand measuring unsteady thrust on a PET bottle and internal air pressure simultaneously.
- A semi-empirical thrust history computed from the pressure record agrees qualitatively with measured thrust.
- High-speed video: air starts to leave before the water is fully discharged; attributed by the authors to swirling flow in the bottle.
- A flow regulator on the nozzle cap suppresses the early air discharge and body vibration.

## What the source supports

From the abstract only:

- A model for the trajectory of a water rocket, simpler than the coupled PDE systems typical of hydrodynamics; presented as a teaching problem a student can complete in a day or two.
- The problem is used to illustrate mass, momentum and energy balances, scale analysis, numerical methods for initial value problems, balances in accelerated frames, and collection and assessment of flight test data.

Statements made about this paper by other sources (not checked against the paper): fischer2020approximation reports that Prusa uses $n = 1.4$ (dry air, p. 724), neglects air-only thrust, and states that thrust from air alone is negligible (p. 719). Not verified against the paper.

## What the source supports

From the abstract only:

- Undergraduate experiment: water rocket on a thrust stand; net thrust, internal air pressure and internal air temperature measured vs time.
- Comparison with an analytical model based on fluid momentum; agreement reported for four cases: two initial water heights times two initial air pressures.
- Air expansion modelled as polytropic; exponent 1.1 gives the best fit.
- At higher air pressure, air mixes with the expelled water (two-phase flow), which reduces net thrust.

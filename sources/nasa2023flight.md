## What the source supports

Qualitative description only. No equations.

- Thrust phase: as water leaves, air volume grows, pressure drops, mass flow rate and thrust drop; weight and thrust change during powered flight.
- Air-only phase: after the water is gone, the residual pressure difference produces a small additional thrust; thrust ends when pressures equalize.
- Coast: ballistic-like trajectory altered by drag; weather-cocking turns the rocket into the wind and lowers maximum altitude.
- Numerical values: 2 l soda bottle; water filled "typically about 40% of the volume". The page does not claim 40% is optimal.

- No optimal fill fraction.
